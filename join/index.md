Applications are accepted at every stage. We keep the process short and clear.

## Graduate students

Apply through the Example University graduate program and mention the lab in your statement. Rotation students are considered.

## Postdoctoral researchers

Send a brief note about what you want to work on, a CV, and the names of two people who can speak about your work. Write to {% include email.html address=site.email %}.

## Undergraduates

We take a few undergraduates each year for a full-year project. Get in touch about six months ahead of when you would like to start.
